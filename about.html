About Joe Holley, MD FACEP FAEMS

Emergency Medical Resources was founded to support excellence in Emergency Medical Services, emergency medicine, protocol development, education, disaster preparedness, and healthcare consultation.

EMR supports EMS agencies, healthcare organizations, educators, and clinicians through medical oversight, educational programs, and operational consultation.
