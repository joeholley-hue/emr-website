About Emergency Medical Resources

Emergency Medical Resources (EMR) provides EMS agencies with expert medical direction, protocol development, education, and operational guidance. Our mission is to support EMS professionals with the tools, training, and leadership needed to deliver exceptional patient care.

Led by Dr. Joe Holley, EMR brings decades of experience in emergency medicine, disaster response, and EMS system leadership. EMR partners with agencies across multiple states to ensure providers have access to evidence‑based protocols, high‑quality continuing education, and operational support.

EMR collaborates with regional and national organizations to advance EMS practice, improve system readiness, and strengthen disaster preparedness. Whether through protocol development, medical oversight, or educational programs, EMR is committed to supporting the EMS community.

For inquiries, collaboration, or agency support, contact us at admin@emergencymedicalresources.com.
