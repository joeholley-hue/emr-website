About Emergency Medical Resources

Emergency Medical Resources (EMR) provides comprehensive EMS medical direction, protocol development, education, and disaster preparedness support for agencies across multiple states. Our mission is to empower EMS professionals with the knowledge, tools, and leadership needed to deliver exceptional patient care in every environment.
