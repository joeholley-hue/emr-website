Paragon Medical Education Group

Emergency Medical Resources is proud to partner with Paragon Medical Education Group, one of the nation’s most respected providers of EMS and medical education. Paragon delivers high‑impact training designed to elevate clinical performance, strengthen operational readiness, and support EMS professionals at every stage of their careers.

Their programs are built on evidence‑based practice, real‑world experience, and a commitment to excellence. Paragon’s instructors bring decades of field, clinical, and leadership expertise, ensuring every learner receives practical, relevant, and immediately applicable education.

Through this partnership, EMR agencies gain access to advanced continuing education, hands‑on workshops, leadership development, and specialized training tailored to the needs of modern EMS systems. Together, EMR and Paragon are committed to strengthening the future of prehospital care.
