Disaster Podcast

The Disaster Podcast delivers real‑world insights into emergency response, disaster medicine, EMS operations, and preparedness. Hosted by experienced responders and medical professionals, the show explores lessons learned from major incidents, emerging threats, and best practices for agencies and providers.

About the Show

Each episode features discussions on disaster response, EMS leadership, medical direction, and operational readiness. Whether you're a paramedic, EMT, emergency manager, or healthcare provider, the Disaster Podcast offers valuable perspectives to strengthen your preparedness and response capabilities.
